AlainPortfolio is an offline Android portfolio app that introduces me to a recruiter in about two minutes. I am a Year 3 Software Engineering student at INES Ruhengeri, and I want to gain web and app development skills that help solve social issues.

## Features

The app has a Home screen with my introduction and verification code, a Skills and education screen, a Projects screen with detail pages for AlainPortfolio and Milk Check, a profile editor with validation and a profile photo from the camera or gallery, and an About screen. Profile data is saved with AsyncStorage and restored after a restart. It is built with React Native, Expo and TypeScript.

## Known limitations

The app works on Android only, the profile photo is stored at low resolution, and skills, education and projects are changed in the source code. Data stays on the device.
